A phone companion that starts with the person, and shows one thing at a time

Ten moments of the Android journey, each drawn at phone size in the approved Routes × UBS identity. Under every screen: what the person is trying to do, the one thing they must understand, and why each element is there.

Branch feature/android-routes-redesign, cut from local main at 0e198b7 (Developer A's evolving-twin core). Numbers in the Alex screens are the values the shared Kotlin engine already asserts in EvolvingTwinTest (CHF 300 worst April, CHF 550 after confirming CHF 1,450). The phone will read them from the engine at runtime, not from these drawings.

Routes is an independent hackathon prototype for the UBS AI Financial Twin challenge. It is not made, approved or endorsed by UBS. No UBS logo, typeface or imagery is used.

Update · 22 Nov · three spaces

Explore, My Routes, My Twin: one session, three views of it

The phone gets three destinations in a bottom bar and nothing else. You talk in Explore, experiment in My Routes and check what Routes recorded in My Twin. All three read and write one shared session, so none of them keeps its own copy of the numbers.

Explore

Talk

Voice first, with a full text alternative. Each supported request becomes an engine command: what-if, correction, rejection, “why”, compare. When a result is calculated, one short finding appears, plus a link to the decision.

My Routes

Experiment

One card per decision that a conversation created. Open it to switch routes, drag the deposit, tap a month, compare two routes, confirm a fact, step back through versions and open the math.

My Twin

Check and correct

What Routes has recorded, grouped by how sure it is: confirmed by you, estimates, assumptions, your commitments, sources, and history. Tap any line to see where it came from, edit it or delete it.

My Twin · confirmed facts and history

ScreenWhat each tap does in the engine
Explore“What if I moved out?” starts a decision graph, which becomes the My Routes card. “Fourteen fifty” is turned into a deposit amount and previewed. Just trying runs explore(WhatIfInput). That's real runs correct(AnswerInput) only after this tap. Rejecting a suggestion out loud is proposed as RejectRepair and saved only once confirmed.
My RoutesThe route control selects a node: the confirmed move, the delay option, or stay. Dragging the deposit previews it (no new version). Keep as a route adds a what-if node. It's the real amount adds a confirmed correction. Tapping a month opens that month's causes and math. 3 versions lists the graph's path and lets you go back to any node. Compare uses compareBranches. Values not computed yet show “…”, never a guess.
My TwinLists inputs and commitments from the active node's session, each with its provenance tag (user-stated, range, assumed, computed, external). History is the graph's list of nodes. Editing a fact is the same correct() call as anywhere else, so the earlier version stays as its parent node.

What the phone does not have that desktop does: several routes side by side with animation. On the phone, Compare shows two routes, one at a time on the same chart. Nothing requires moving to the laptop to finish an analysis.
